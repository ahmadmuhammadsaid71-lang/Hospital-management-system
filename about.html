Who We Are

CarePlus Hospital is an imaginary healthcare organization created for the CSC 210 Web Application Development project. The hospital is designed to provide patients with access to different healthcare services in a welcoming environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible, reliable and compassionate healthcare services while treating every patient with dignity and respect.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted healthcare centre known for quality service, professionalism and patient satisfaction.
